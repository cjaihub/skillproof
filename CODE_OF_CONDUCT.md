# Code of conduct

SkillProof exists to serve refugees and displaced people. Everyone who takes part in this project (in code, issues,
translations or discussions) is expected to treat others with the same dignity the platform is built to protect.

## Expected

- Be respectful, patient and welcoming, especially to people writing in a second or third language.
- Assume good intent; critique ideas and code, never people.
- Protect privacy: never post anyone's personal data, documents, or identifying details from refugee settings.
- Credit others' work, including translators and community reviewers.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or discrimination based on nationality, ethnicity, religion, gender, disability, legal status or any other characteristic.
- Stereotyping or demeaning language about refugees, migrants or host communities.
- Sharing other people's private information without explicit permission.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content and block people who break this code. To report a problem privately, contact
the maintainer through GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially.
