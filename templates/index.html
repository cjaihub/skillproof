How it works

  1. RegisterCreate your account. Your data stays private until you choose to share.
  2. Build your skills profileList your skills, level and work experience, including informal work.
  3. Submit evidenceUpload whatever you still have: certificates, photos of work, code, references.
  4. Get assessedComplete a short practical task. A trained assessor scores it against clear criteria.
  5. Share your reportGet a digital competency report with a link you control, and you can switch it off any time.

Honest by design

Self-declared skills and independently assessed ones are always shown separately, so employers know exactly what has been checked.

Fair and accountable

Every decision records who assessed it, the scores and the reasons. You can appeal, and a different assessor will reassess.

Your data, your choice

Built with Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019 in mind: consent before sharing, access logs, data export and deletion.
