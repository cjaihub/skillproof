Privacy & data protection

Prototype notice, designed with Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019 in mind. Have it reviewed by a qualified data protection adviser before a live launch.

What we collect and why

  • Account details (name, email, password stored only as a secure hash), so you can log in.
  • Your skills profile and work history, to describe your competencies.
  • Evidence you upload (certificates, work samples, links), so assessors can evaluate your skills.
  • Assessment results, to produce your competency report.

We only collect what is needed for skills recognition (data minimisation). Please do not upload refugee ID numbers, passport scans or health information. They are not needed.

Your rights

  • Consent: sharing is off by default. You can turn it on or off at any time, and create a new link to cancel the old one.
  • Access: see who viewed your profile and evidence in your access log, and download all your data as a file.
  • Correction: edit your profile at any time, and appeal any assessment decision you believe is wrong.
  • Deletion: delete your account and all your data, including uploaded files, from your account page.

How we protect it

  • Passwords are salted and hashed, never stored in plain text.
  • Evidence files are stored outside the public website with random names, and are only served to you, assessors and administrators after a permission check.
  • Role-based access control separates candidates, assessors, employers and administrators.
  • An audit log records assessments, role changes, consent changes and every time someone views your data.
