# User guide

## Candidates

1. **Register**: choose *I'm looking for work*, enter your details, set a password, agree to the privacy notice. Confirm your email.
2. **Build your profile**: headline, location, a short summary and your work history. The dashboard shows how complete it is.
   - Shortcut: **Import with AI**. Upload a CV, certificate or photo of your work, then tick the suggestions you want to keep.
3. **Add skills**: name, category, your level (Beginner to Expert) and years of experience.
4. **Add evidence**: photos of your work, references, portfolios, links. *Lost your certificates?* Describe the work you
   did, ask a former colleague or supervisor for a short reference, or photograph things you built or repaired.
   **Never upload ID or refugee registration documents.**
5. **Take the skills test** for a skill (6 questions plus 2 short answers), then answer the **practical task**, and **submit for assessment**.
6. You'll get a notification when an assessor decides. Verified skills get a badge and a score out of 20. If you
   disagree, **appeal** with your reasons; a different assessor may look again.
7. **Share your report**: open *Competency report*, give consent, and copy your link. You can share one competence area, stop sharing,
   or change the link at any time.
8. **Get job-ready**: with a verified skill, a confirmed email, a 70%+ profile and sharing on, request job access. An admin reviews it.
9. **Jobs**: browse jobs sorted by how well they match your skills, apply with a short message, and track your progress in *My applications*.

## Assessors

1. Register as an assessor; an admin approves you.
2. **Assessment queue**: skills waiting longest come first, and late ones are flagged.
3. Open a skill: read the evidence, test results and practical answer. Optionally click **AI review** for suggested
   scores, concerns and questions to ask. It's advice only.
4. Score each criterion from 0 to 5: **knowledge, practical ability, evidence quality, communication**. A skill passes at **12/20 with
   no criterion below 2**. Set the level you saw, write clear feedback, confirm you have no conflict of interest, and save.

## Employers

1. Register as an employer; an admin approves your organisation.
2. **Find talent**: search by keyword, category, location and level, or show verified skills only. Open a report and shortlist people with a private note.
3. **Post a job**: an admin approves it before it goes live, and job-ready candidates who match are told about it.
4. **Applicants**: each shows a match score and their report. Move people through Shortlisted → Interview → Offer → Hired.
5. Click **Translate** to read profiles and notes written in another language.

> Never ask candidates for money, ID numbers or bank details. Doing so gets an account removed.

## Admins

- **Dashboard**: platform health, growth, assessment funnel, workload, live activity.
- **Verifications**: approve or reject employers and assessors, verify identities, and grant or decline job access.
- **Users**: search, filter, bulk approve or suspend, change role, sign a user out everywhere, export CSV.
- **Jobs**: approve or reject job ads, with a reason.
- **Appeals**: uphold (sends the skill to a new assessor) or reject, with a written reason.
- **Audit log** and **Settings**.

## Everyone

- **Language**: use the globe button. Your choice is remembered.
- **Theme**: Light, Dark or System.
- **Need help?** The assistant answers in your language and knows your next step.
- **Account**: change your password, see sign-in history, download your data, or delete your account.
